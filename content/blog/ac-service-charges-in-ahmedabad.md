If you have searched for **AC service charges in Ahmedabad**, you have probably seen prices from ₹299 to well over ₹1,500 for what sounds like the same job. The difference is usually in what is included, not in the label on the booking. This guide breaks down typical 2026 prices for split AC service, gas refill, installation and common repairs in Ahmedabad, so you know what is reasonable before a technician arrives.

## Split AC service charges in Ahmedabad: basic vs jet service

Most households in Ahmedabad book one of two services:

**Basic (dry) service.** The technician removes and washes the filters, cleans the front of the indoor coil, checks the drain and measures cooling. It is fine as a mid-season top-up, but it does not reach the dust packed deep inside the coil and blower.

**Jet service (deep cleaning).** A water jet pump is used to wash the indoor coil and blower wheel while a bag catches the dirty water, and the outdoor condenser is cleaned too. Because Ahmedabad gets so much fine dust, especially in homes near main roads or construction, a jet service once a year usually makes the biggest difference to cooling and power bills. **AC jet service charges** in the city are typically ₹600 to ₹1,000 per split unit.

## AC gas refill price in Ahmedabad (by tonnage)

The **AC gas refill price in Ahmedabad** depends on three things:

1. **Tonnage:** a 1.5 ton AC holds more refrigerant than a 1 ton unit, so it costs more to fill.
2. **Gas type:** most newer inverter ACs use R32; older units may use R22 or R410A. R410A is generally the costliest to recharge.
3. **Leak repair:** refrigerant does not get used up. If your AC needs gas, it is leaking somewhere. A good technician finds and fixes the leak first (brazing a joint, replacing a flare nut or valve), which can add ₹300 to ₹1,500.

For a **1.5 ton AC gas refill in Ahmedabad**, expect roughly ₹2,200 to ₹3,200 including the leak test. Be wary of very cheap "top-ups" with no leak test; the gas often escapes again within weeks. If you are not sure gas is the problem, see our checklist of [signs your AC needs a gas refill](/blog/signs-your-ac-needs-a-gas-refill).

## AC installation charges in Ahmedabad

Standard split AC installation is usually ₹1,200 to ₹1,800 per unit. The quote often looks low because it covers only the labour. Ask about:

- **Copper pipe:** charged per foot beyond the length supplied with the AC.
- **Outdoor stand:** needed if the unit cannot sit on a ledge or the roof.
- **Core cutting:** drilling through a thick wall for the pipe.
- **Electrical work:** a dedicated MCB or new wiring for the AC point.

On high floors and roof-mounted outdoor units, the condenser should be placed where it gets airflow and, ideally, some afternoon shade. That matters a lot in Ahmedabad's summer.

## What makes AC service more expensive?

- **Hard-to-reach outdoor units** that need a ladder, rope or extra helper
- **Very dirty or neglected ACs** that need chemical cleaning
- **Brand-specific parts**, especially for inverter models
- **Peak season urgency**: same-day visits in May are in high demand
- **Multiple small faults** found during the service

## How to avoid overpaying for AC service in Ahmedabad

1. **Ask what is included** before booking: filter only, or a full jet wash of the coil and outdoor unit?
2. **Insist on a pressure reading** before paying for gas, and ask for a leak test.
3. **Ask for old parts back** when something is replaced.
4. **Get a written or WhatsApp quote** with labour and parts listed separately.
5. **Book before the rush.** February and March are the best months for pre-summer servicing in Ahmedabad.
6. **Consider an AMC** if you have two or more ACs. Annual maintenance plans usually cost less than booking each service separately.
