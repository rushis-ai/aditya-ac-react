Most people only think about AC service when the cooling stops. In Ahmedabad that usually happens in the first heatwave of April, exactly when every technician in the city is fully booked. So **how often should an AC be serviced** here, and when is the right time? Here is a practical schedule for Ahmedabad's climate, plus a checklist of what a good service should include.

## The short answer

| Usage | Recommended schedule |
|---|---|
| Typical home (summer use, 6–10 hrs/day) | **2 services a year:** deep jet service in Feb–Mar, basic service in Oct–Nov |
| Heavy use (shops, offices, bedrooms used year-round) | **3–4 services a year** |
| Homes near main roads or construction | 2–3 services a year, plus filter cleaning every 2 weeks |
| Filter cleaning (DIY) | Every 2–4 weeks while the AC is in regular use |

## Why Ahmedabad ACs need more frequent servicing

- **Dust:** fine dust from roads, construction and dry winds settles on the indoor coil and outdoor condenser, reducing airflow and heat transfer.
- **Long, extreme summers:** ACs here run for months at a stretch with afternoons above 40°C, which wears motors and capacitors faster.
- **Monsoon humidity:** after the rains, damp coils and drain trays can grow mould and cause musty smells and water leakage.
- **Pigeons and debris:** outdoor units on balconies and terraces often collect feathers, nesting material and leaves.

## Is it necessary to service an AC before summer?

Yes, and timing matters. The ideal window in Ahmedabad is **mid-February to mid-March**:

- The weather is still mild, so any repairs are not an emergency.
- Technicians have availability, so you are not waiting days in April.
- Gas leaks, weak capacitors and failing fan motors get caught before they fail at 45°C.
- A clean AC uses less electricity through the most expensive months.

Skipping the **AC service before summer** is the main reason we see ACs struggling or tripping in peak heat. If your AC is already struggling, see [why ACs stop cooling in 45°C heat](/blog/ac-not-cooling-in-45-degrees).

## What is an AC jet service?

A **jet service** is a deep clean using a high-pressure water jet pump. The technician covers the indoor unit's electronics, fits a collection bag underneath, and jet-washes the cooling coil and blower wheel to flush out packed-in dust and grime. The outdoor condenser is cleaned as well. It removes far more dirt than a basic filter-and-brush service, which is why it is the best choice for the main pre-summer service. ## Pre-summer AC service checklist

A complete service should include:

1. **Filter removal and wash**
2. **Jet cleaning of the indoor coil and blower wheel**
3. **Outdoor condenser cleaning** (fins straightened if bent)
4. **Drain pipe and tray flushing** to prevent water leakage
5. **Gas pressure check**, with a leak test if pressure is low
6. **Capacitor and fan motor check**
7. **Electrical connections and voltage check**
8. **Remote, sensors and display check**
9. **Cooling test:** temperature difference between air entering and leaving the indoor unit
10. **Report:** what was found, and anything that may need attention soon

Ask your technician to show you the before-and-after condition of the filters and coil.

## How long does AC service take?

- **Basic service:** about 30–45 minutes per split AC
- **Jet service:** about 60–90 minutes per split AC, including the outdoor unit
- **Gas refill with leak repair:** add 45–90 minutes

For homes with three or four ACs, plan for half a day, or ask about an annual maintenance plan (AMC) so services are scheduled for you.

## Signs your AC needs service now

Do not wait for the schedule if you notice:

- Weak cooling or the room takes much longer to cool
- Water dripping from the indoor unit
- A musty or burning smell
- Ice on the copper pipes
- Unusual noises, rattling or clicking
- A sudden jump in your electricity bill

## Things you can do between services

- Wash the filters every 2–4 weeks in summer.
- Keep 1–2 feet of clear space around the outdoor unit.
- Use 24–26°C as your default temperature.
- Run the AC in Fan mode for 10 minutes before switching off after the monsoon, to dry the coil and prevent smells.
- Cover the outdoor unit only when the AC is not in use for months, and remove the cover before switching on.
